The problem

Three tools. Zero connection. Missed placements.

LinkedIn for research. Outlook for email. HubSpot for tracking. Nothing talks to each other, and you're the one manually copying data between them, until you forget.

You forget to follow up

You send a cold email, log it nowhere, and by the time you remember, the hiring manager moved on to someone else.

Manual data entry kills time

Copy name from LinkedIn. Paste into CRM. Find email separately. Add job title. You spend 20 minutes doing what should take 20 seconds.

Generic follow-ups get ignored

Your CRM sends the same template to everyone. Hiring managers can tell immediately. Hirely writes follow-ups based on your actual email. They sound like you.

How it works

From LinkedIn to follow-up in under 30 seconds

Use the beta Chrome extension on LinkedIn, then manage follow-ups, emails, and drafts in the Hirely dashboard.

1

Browse LinkedIn

Visit any HR manager or hiring contact's LinkedIn profile. The Hirely panel opens automatically on the right.

2

One click to save

Hit Save. Name, title, company, and LinkedIn URL captured instantly. No typing. Panel shows if they're already in your pipeline.

3

Search when you need an email

Click Find email in the dashboard. One credit is used only when an address is returned; failed and no-result searches use zero.

4

AI writes follow-ups

Click Generate to prepare 3 personalized follow-up drafts. Review and edit a draft, then open it in your email app.

Features

Everything a recruiter actually needs

Built for technical and industrial staffing recruiters in Canada. Not another generic CRM.

Chrome extension

Floating panel on LinkedIn. Knows if you've saved someone before, shows their pipeline status, saves with one click.

Email enrichment

Request work email searches when needed. Hirely separates verified results from predictions and charges only when an address is returned.

AI follow-up drafts

Generates 3 follow-up emails per contact based on your original outreach. Sounds like you, not a template.

Recruiter pipeline

Three clear stages: Follow up today, Coming up, and Done. Choose follow-up dates and keep notes and activity together.

Company page intel

On any LinkedIn company page, Hirely shows company info: industry, size, location, founded year, pulled from People Data Labs.

Analytics dashboard

Enrichment rate, reply rate, top companies, pipeline funnel, and monthly growth, all in one view.
